Cozy Space Backdrops — pixel art space pack

8 planets, each with a 24-frame rotation sheet, 8 asteroids, 3 parallax star
layers and 3 nebula backgrounds. Every background tile repeats seamlessly.

Folders:
  planets/1x, 2x, 4x      planet-<type>.png               one frame
                          planet-<type>-spin-24f.png      24 frames in a row, loops
  asteroids/1x, 2x, 4x    asteroid-1..8.png               16 px and 24 px
  backgrounds/1x, 2x      stars-far / stars-mid / stars-near-tile.png   128 px
                          nebula-violet / teal / rose-tile.png          256 px

Planet types: terran, desert, ice, lava, toxic, moon, gas, gasblue (ringed).
Spin sheets: 48 x 48 px frames at 1x (the ringed planet is 82 x 48),
suggested speed 8-12 fps. Scale with nearest-neighbor to keep pixels crisp.

Parallax: put stars-far (opaque) at the back and scroll stars-mid and
stars-near (transparent) faster on top. The nebula tiles already include
a few stars and work on their own.

Made to match Cozy Space Station and Walking Crew (aquumgifts.itch.io).
